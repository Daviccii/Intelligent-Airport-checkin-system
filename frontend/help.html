Flight Delays & Cancellations

Your Rights:

If your flight is delayed or cancelled, you may be entitled to compensation, meals, accommodation, and rebooking options depending on the circumstances.

What to Do if Your Flight is Delayed:

  • Check flight status: Use our real-time flight tracker or mobile app
  • Stay informed: Monitor airport displays and check your email/SMS for updates
  • Contact us: Our customer service team is available 24/7
  • Know your rights: Delays over 3 hours may qualify for compensation

Compensation Guidelines:

  • Delays 2-3 hours: Meals and refreshments
  • Delays 3-5 hours: Meals, refreshments, and communication facilities
  • Delays over 5 hours: Full refund or alternative flight, plus accommodation if overnight
  • Cancellations: Rebooking on next available flight or full refund
