Baggage Allowance & Fees

Use this page to understand baggage allowances. Exact allowances depend on fare class and route.

Typical Allowances

  • Economy: 1 carry-on + 1 checked bag (varies by fare)
  • Premium Economy: 1 carry-on + 2 checked bags
  • Business: 2 carry-on + 2-3 checked bags

For exact baggage charges and to purchase extras, use the Buy Extras option on bookings.
