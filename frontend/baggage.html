Baggage Information & Services

Plan Your Baggage Ahead

Understanding baggage allowances and restrictions helps ensure a smooth journey. Review our comprehensive baggage guide below.

✈️ Hand Baggage (Carry-On)

All passengers are allowed to bring hand baggage into the cabin, subject to size and weight restrictions:

Class Number of Pieces Weight per Piece Dimensions (L+W+H)
Economy 1 carry-on + 1 personal item 7 kg (15 lbs) 115 cm (45 inches)
Premium Economy 1 carry-on + 1 personal item 10 kg (22 lbs) 115 cm (45 inches)
Business Class 2 carry-ons + 1 personal item 14 kg (30 lbs) total 115 cm (45 inches) each
📱 Personal Items Include:
  • Handbag or small backpack
  • Laptop bag
  • Briefcase
  • Camera bag
🚫 Not Allowed in Cabin:
  • Sharp objects (scissors, knives)
  • Liquids over 100ml
  • Flammable materials
  • Sporting equipment

🐾 Traveling with Pets

We understand pets are family. SmartFly welcomes small pets in the cabin and larger pets in cargo:

Cabin Travel (Small Pets)

  • Eligible: Dogs and cats under 8 kg (17 lbs) including carrier
  • Carrier Size: Max 45cm x 35cm x 25cm
  • Fee: $75-150 USD depending on route
  • Limit: One pet per passenger

Requirements: Valid health certificate, up-to-date vaccinations, soft-sided carrier that fits under seat.

Cargo Travel (Large Pets)

  • Eligible: Dogs and cats over 8 kg
  • Container: IATA-approved pet carrier
  • Fee: $200-500 USD depending on weight and route
  • Climate Control: Temperature-controlled cargo hold

Requirements: Microchip, import permits, rabies vaccination, veterinary health certificate issued within 10 days of travel.

⚠️ Important Notes:
  • Book pet travel at least 7 days in advance
  • Some countries have quarantine requirements
  • Certain breeds may have travel restrictions
  • Service animals travel free in cabin with proper documentation

⚠️ Baggage Restrictions & Prohibited Items

🚫 Prohibited in Checked & Carry-On

  • Explosives and fireworks
  • Flammable liquids and solids
  • Compressed gases
  • Corrosive materials
  • Radioactive materials
  • Strong magnetic materials
  • Lithium batteries (loose)
  • Toxic substances

⚠️ Restricted Items

  • Liquids in carry-on (max 100ml per container)
  • Sharp objects (checked baggage only)
  • Sports equipment (must be declared)
  • Power banks (carry-on only, max 100Wh)
  • E-cigarettes (carry-on only)
  • Alcoholic beverages (max 5L, 24-70% ABV)

✅ Special Items

  • Musical instruments (extra seat purchase available)
  • Sporting equipment (golf clubs, skis)
  • Medical equipment (pre-notification required)
  • Wheelchairs and mobility aids (free of charge)
  • Infant car seats and strollers (free)

🔍 Lost or Delayed Baggage

While we handle millions of bags annually with care, occasionally baggage may be delayed or misplaced. Here's what to do:

Immediate Steps at Airport

  1. Report immediately at the baggage service desk before leaving the airport
  2. File a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) - you'll receive a reference number
  3. Provide detailed description of your baggage (color, brand, distinctive features)
  4. Keep your baggage claim tag and boarding pass for verification
  5. Request emergency kit if baggage is delayed overnight

Delayed Baggage

Most delayed bags arrive within 24-48 hours

  • Track online with PIR number
  • We'll deliver to your address when found
  • Emergency toiletries provided
  • Reimbursement for essential purchases (keep receipts)

Lost/Damaged Baggage

If baggage is not located after 21 days:

  • File compensation claim within 7 days
  • Provide itemized list of contents
  • Maximum liability: ~$1,700 USD (Montreal Convention)
  • Consider travel insurance for valuable items
