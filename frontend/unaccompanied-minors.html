Unaccompanied Minors Service

Safe, dedicated care for young travelers

👧 SmartFly Unaccompanied Minor Program

SmartFly offers comprehensive services for children traveling alone. Our dedicated staff ensures your child is well cared for from check-in to arrival, providing a safe and comfortable journey with peace of mind for parents.

👩‍⚖️ Eligibility & Age Guidelines

Unaccompanied Minor Service Available: Ages 5-17 years

Age 5-8: Mandatory unaccompanied minor service required. Child must be escorted through entire journey.

Age 9-11: Service recommended but optional. Parent can choose to have staff supervision.

Age 12-17: Service optional. Some restrictions apply for very long flights or multiple connections.

🌟 Our Unaccompanied Minor Services

💳 Check-in Assistance

  • Direct check-in at desk
  • Guardian escorted to gate
  • Luggage handled personally
  • Documentation reviewed

🌟 Boarding Priority

  • Priority boarding first
  • Staff escorts to seat
  • Seat location chosen for safety
  • Special attention from crew

🍽️ In-Flight Care

  • Regular crew check-ins
  • Meals provided
  • Entertainment support
  • Lavatory assistance if needed

📞 Communication

  • Updates to parent/guardian
  • Emergency contact available
  • Regular check-in calls
  • 24/7 support line

🚶 Connection Support

  • Gate-to-gate assistance
  • Guided through airport
  • Baggage tracked personally
  • Re-boarding priority

📩 Arrival Handover

  • Meet at luggage carousel
  • Identification verification
  • Direct handover to guardian
  • Confirmation to parent

📄 Required Documentation

Parent/Guardian must provide:

  • Child's valid passport or ID
  • Parental consent form (signed)
  • Contact details of receiving guardian
  • Emergency contact information
  • Copies of custody documents (if applicable)
  • Medical information or allergies
  • Insurance documents

📈 Booking Process

  1. Select flight and add "Unaccompanied Minor Service" during booking
  2. Provide child and guardian details
  3. Upload required documentation
  4. Receive confirmation and special instructions
  5. SmartFly team contacts you 48 hours before departure
  6. Parent confirms details and emergency contacts
  7. Child checks in at designated desk

💰 Service Pricing

Unaccompanied Minor Service Fee: KES 1,500 per flight (one-way)

Included in service: Staff escort, meals, priority handling, communication with parents, emergency support

Optional add-ons: Meet & greet (KES 3,500), lounge access (KES 1,500), special meals (KES 500)

❌ Restrictions & Important Rules

  • No connecting flights exceeding 4 hours (requires staff availability)
  • No overnight connections without special arrangements
  • Receiving guardian must have valid ID matching documents
  • Receiving guardian cannot be more than one person (except with prior approval)
  • International flights require passport stamps and entry authorization
  • No service for flights departing after 10pm or before 6am

🙏 Parent/Guardian Responsibilities

  • Ensure child is prepared for solo travel
  • Provide clear instructions to child about flight
  • Ensure child has valuables and medications secured
  • Verify receiving guardian can attend arrival
  • Keep phone available for updates from SmartFly
  • Confirm documentation is complete before booking
  • Inform child of emergency contact procedures
