Seat FAQs

Can I change my seat after booking?

Yes, you can change your seat anytime before check-in through our Manage Booking portal.

Are seats guaranteed?

Once selected and paid for, your seat is guaranteed for your flight.

Can I select seats for my entire family?

Yes, you can select adjacent seats for all passengers in your booking.
