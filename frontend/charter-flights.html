📄 Charter Booking Process

  1. Contact SmartFly Charter specialists with your requirements
  2. Provide travel dates, passenger count, and special needs
  3. Receive aircraft options and availability
  4. Review detailed pricing quote
  5. Confirm choice and provide passenger manifest
  6. Execute charter agreement and payment
  7. Final coordination meeting 7 days before departure
  8. Check-in at private terminals (1 hour before departure)
  9. Enjoy seamless charter experience
