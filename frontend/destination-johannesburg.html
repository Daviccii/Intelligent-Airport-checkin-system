🎯 Top Attractions

Apartheid Museum

Moving journey through South Africa's history of segregation and the triumph of human spirit.

🏘️

Soweto Township

Visit Vilakazi Street, the only street where two Nobel Prize winners lived - Mandela and Tutu.

🎢

Gold Reef City

Theme park built on old gold mine with thrilling rides and gold mining tours.

🏛️

Constitution Hill

Former prison turned museum, where Mandela and Gandhi were once incarcerated.

🌳

Lion & Safari Park

Get close to lions, cheetahs, and giraffes just 40 minutes from the city center.

🛍️

Maboneng Precinct

Trendy arts district with galleries, restaurants, rooftop bars, and street markets.

🚗 Getting Around

  • 🚄 Gautrain: Fast, modern train connecting airport, Sandton, Pretoria, and Rosebank
  • 🚕 Uber & Bolt: Most convenient and safe way to get around the city
  • 🚗 Car Rental: Best for exploring wider areas and flexibility
  • 🚌 Bus Rapid Transit: Rea Vaya buses connect key areas

💡 Travel Tips

  • ✓ Most nationalities receive 90-day visa-free entry at O.R. Tambo Airport
  • ✓ Use Uber/Bolt rather than street taxis for safety
  • ✓ Altitude is 1,753m - you may feel breathless initially
  • ✓ Tipping is customary - 10-15% in restaurants
  • ✓ Exchange rate is favorable - your money goes far here
  • ✓ Weather: warm days, cool nights - bring layers
