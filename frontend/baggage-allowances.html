Checked Baggage Allowances

Comprehensive baggage information for all fare classes

📦 Checked Baggage Overview

Checked baggage is the luggage you hand over at check-in for stowing in the aircraft hold. Baggage allowance depends on your fare class, route, and loyalty status. All bags must have ID tags for safe handling.

📏 Standard Baggage Dimensions & Weight

Economy Class

Max 80 × 60 × 35 cm

Weight: 23 kg (50.7 lbs) per bag

Quantity: 1 bag included

Extra bags: KES 5,000 - 8,000 each

Premium Economy

Max 80 × 60 × 35 cm

Weight: 2 × 23 kg (50.7 lbs)

Quantity: 2 bags included

Extra bags: KES 4,500 - 7,500 each

Business Class

Max 80 × 60 × 35 cm

Weight: 2 × 32 kg (70.5 lbs)

Quantity: 2 bags included

Extra bags: 1 complimentary, then KES 4,000

First Class

Max 80 × 60 × 35 cm

Weight: 3 × 32 kg (70.5 lbs)

Quantity: 3 bags included

Extra bags: All complimentary

📋 What to Pack in Checked Baggage

Allowed items:

  • Clothing and shoes
  • Toiletries and cosmetics (in reasonable quantities)
  • Shoes and belts
  • Books and documents
  • Souvenirs and gifts (non-hazardous)
  • Electronics and cables
  • Sporting equipment (subject to restrictions)

⚠️ Prohibited Items in Checked Baggage

Dangerous goods not permitted:

  • Explosives and flammables
  • Weapons and firearms
  • Corrosive substances
  • Gases (compressed/liquefied)
  • Radioactive materials
  • Biological hazards
  • Lithium batteries (with exceptions)
  • Perfume (more than 2 liters)
  • Wet paint and varnish
  • Sharp objects not properly protected

🔍 Baggage Claim & Damage

Baggage claim process:

  1. Proceed to baggage claim area after arrival
  2. Collect your bags from the carousel
  3. Inspect for damage before leaving airport
  4. Report any damage to baggage services immediately

Liability: SmartFly maintains comprehensive baggage liability insurance. Maximum compensation per bag: USD 3,000 (or equivalent in local currency)

🛄 Special Baggage Services

Fragile items handling: KES 2,000 - premium care and special packaging

Oversized items: Golf bags, ski equipment, surfboards - KES 3,500 per item

Priority baggage: Guaranteed first off carousel - KES 1,500

Unaccompanied baggage: Available for separate shipment with proper documentation
