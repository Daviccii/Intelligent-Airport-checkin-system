Your Rights in Case of Delay or Cancellation

\n
\n
\n

🔄 Rebooking

\n

Rebooking on the next available flight at no additional cost

\n
\n
\n

💰 Compensation

\n

EU 261 compensation up to €600 for eligible flights

\n
\n
\n

🏨 Accommodation

\n

Hotel and meal vouchers for lengthy delays

\n
\n
\n

📞 Communication

\n

Free phone calls, emails, and fax communications

\n
\n
\n

🚗 Transport

\n

Transport to accommodation and airport if needed

\n
\n
\n

💵 Refund Option

\n

Full refund if you choose not to travel

\n
\n
\n
\n\n

Flight Delayed 3+ Hours

\n

What you're entitled to:

\n

✓ Meals and refreshments
✓ Communication facilities
✓ Hotel accommodation (if overnight)
✓ Transport to/from airport

\n
\n\n
\n

Flight Delayed 5+ Hours

\n

What you're entitled to:

\n

✓ All above benefits
✓ Full refund
✓ Rebooking on alternative flight
✓ Compensation (eligible flights)

\n
\n\n
\n

Flight Cancelled

\n

What you're entitled to:

\n

✓ Rebooking without charge
✓ Full refund
✓ Care and assistance
✓ Compensation (if within EU)
