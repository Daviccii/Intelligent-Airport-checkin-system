🚗 Getting Around

  • 🚇 Metro: 9 lines covering all major districts - clean, modern, and efficient
  • 🚌 Buses: Extensive network with numbered routes - affordable and reliable
  • 🚕 Taxis: Affordable metered taxis throughout the city
  • 🚲 Bike-Sharing: Mobike and Ofo services available at low costs

💡 Travel Tips

  • ✓ Visa requirements - check if you need Chinese visa or visa-free entry
  • ✓ WeChat Pay and Alipay essential - cash usage limited in modern areas
  • ✓ English not widely spoken - download translation apps
  • ✓ Bargaining expected in markets and malls
  • ✓ Summer (May-Sep) very hot and humid - avoid if possible
  • ✓ Tap water not safe to drink - use bottled water
