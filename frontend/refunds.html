How to Request a Refund

1

Find Your Booking

Log in to your account or enter your booking reference and last name to access your reservation details.

2

Request Cancellation

Click "Cancel Booking" and select your reason for cancellation. Review the refund amount before confirming.

3

Review & Confirm

Check your refund eligibility, amount, and processing time. Accept the terms and submit your request.

4

Await Processing

We'll review your request within 24 hours. You'll receive a confirmation email with your refund reference number.

5

Receive Refund

Your refund will be processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. Track status online.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to receive my refund?

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days. Depending on your bank or payment provider, it may take an additional 3-5 days to appear in your account.

Can I get a refund if I missed my flight?

If you missed your flight due to circumstances within your control, refunds are typically not available. However, you may be able to pay a rebooking fee to catch the next available flight.

What happens if SmartFly cancels my flight?

If we cancel or significantly delay your flight, you're entitled to a full refund or free rebooking to an alternative flight. We'll also provide compensation according to applicable regulations.

Can I transfer my travel credit to someone else?

Yes! Travel credits can be transferred to immediate family members (spouse, children, parents, siblings). Contact customer service with both parties' details to process the transfer.

Do I get a refund for taxes and fees?

Yes, government taxes and airport fees are fully refundable regardless of ticket type, as these charges are only payable when you actually fly.

Can I cancel just one passenger on a group booking?

Yes, you can cancel individual passengers from a group booking. Each passenger's refund will be calculated based on their specific ticket type and cancellation timing.
