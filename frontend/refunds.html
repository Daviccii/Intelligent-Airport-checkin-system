Refunds & Credits

\n

Understand our refund policy and process

\n
\n\n
\n
\n
\n

💳 Refundable Tickets

\n

Full refund if cancelled 7+ days before departure

\n
\n
\n

🎫 Non-Refundable Tickets

\n

Travel credit issued; no cash refund available

\n
\n
\n

✈️ Schedule Change

\n

Automatic refund if we reschedule your flight

\n
\n
\n

⏰ Processing Time

\n

5-7 business days for refund to appear

\n
\n
\n\n
\n

Refund Process

\n \n
\n
1
\n
\n

Request Cancellation

\n

Submit your cancellation request via our website, app, or customer service within the allowed timeframe.

\n
\n
\n\n
\n
2
\n
\n

Review Eligibility

\n

We'll review your ticket type and cancellation window to determine refund eligibility.

\n
\n
\n\n
\n
3
\n
\n

Calculate Amount

\n

Deductibles and service fees (if applicable) will be subtracted from your refund amount.

\n
\n
\n\n
\n
4
\n
\n

Issue Refund

\n

Refund will be issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

\n
\n
\n\n
\n
5
\n
\n

Confirmation Email

\n

You'll receive a confirmation email with refund details and tracking information.

\n
\n
\n
\n\n
\n
\n

Full Refund

\n

Available for cancellations made 7 or more days before departure. 100% of the ticket fare is refunded.

\n
\n\n
\n

Partial Refund

\n

Available for cancellations made 3-7 days before departure. Admin fees and service charges apply.

\n
\n\n
\n

Travel Credit

\n

Non-refundable tickets can be converted to travel credit valid for 12 months from original departure date.

\n
\n\n
\n

Schedule Change

\n

If we change your flight schedule, you're eligible for a full refund or rebooking at no additional cost.
