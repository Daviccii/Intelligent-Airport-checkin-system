About SmartFly Airways

Connecting Africa to the world since 2001

Our Story

Founded in 2001, SmartFly Airways emerged from a vision to connect East Africa with the world through safe, reliable, and customer-focused air travel. Starting with a single Boeing 737 operating regional routes within Kenya, we have grown into a respected carrier serving destinations across Africa, Europe, and the Middle East.

Today, with a modern fleet of 24 aircraft and over 1,200 dedicated employees, SmartFly Airways carries more than 3 million passengers annually. Our commitment to excellence, safety, and innovation has made us a preferred airline for both business and leisure travelers.
