Travel Requirements

Essential documents and information for your journey

🛂 Passport & Visa Requirements

Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond your intended date of departure. Different countries require different visa types. Check requirements based on your nationality and destination.

Common Visa Categories

  • Tourist Visa - For holiday and leisure travel
  • Business Visa - For business meetings and conferences
  • Transit Visa - For connecting flights through a country
  • Residence Permit - For long-term stays
  • eVisa/eTA - Electronic travel authorization (available for many destinations)

🆔 Required Travel Documents

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity)
  • Visa or travel authorization (if required)
  • Boarding pass (printed or mobile)
  • Travel insurance documents (recommended)
  • Vaccination certificates (if applicable)
  • Return ticket or onward flight confirmation
  • Hotel booking confirmation
  • Proof of financial means (credit cards, bank statements)

📋 Document Preparation Checklist

2-3 months before travel: Check visa requirements for your destination

6-8 weeks before: Apply for visa if needed

2-4 weeks before: Confirm passport validity and arrange travel insurance

1 week before: Print/download boarding pass, confirm hotel bookings

Day before: Pack documents in easily accessible location

🌍 Popular Destinations - Visa Requirements

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

Visa Required: Yes (for most nationalities)

Processing Time: 4-8 weeks

Validity: 6 months standard visit

🇫🇷 France

Visa Required: Schengen visa (for non-EU)

Processing Time: 2-4 weeks

Validity: Depends on visa type

🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates

Visa Required: Yes (often issued on arrival)

Processing Time: Instant on arrival

Validity: 30 days visit visa

🇹🇭 Thailand

Visa Required: Varies by nationality

Processing Time: 1-2 weeks

Validity: 60-90 days typically

🇺🇸 United States

Visa Required: Yes (ESTA for visa-waiver countries)

Processing Time: ESTA instant, Visa 1-3 months

Validity: B1/B2 visa 10 years

🇨🇳 China

Visa Required: Yes (mandatory)

Processing Time: 4-15 working days

Validity: 30-180 days

⚠️ Health & Safety Requirements

  • Yellow Fever vaccination (required for certain destinations)
  • Malaria prevention medication (if traveling to endemic areas)
  • Travel health insurance recommended
  • Check disease advisories with your health provider
  • Carry prescription medications in original bottles
  • Medical documentation for special medications

💰 Financial Requirements

Some countries may ask for proof of sufficient funds. Common requirements:

  • Bank statements showing adequate balance
  • Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard widely accepted)
  • Travel money card or prepaid card
  • Traveler's cheques (less common)
  • At least $1,000-2,000 USD equivalent for standard 2-week trip

⏰ Important Reminders

Passport Validity: Must be valid 6+ months from departure date

Blank Pages: Ensure your passport has blank pages for visa stamps (usually 2+ pages required)

Visa Processing: Apply early - processing times vary significantly

Changes: Regulations change frequently - verify requirements 4-6 weeks before travel
