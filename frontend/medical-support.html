🏥 Medical Conditions & Flying Restrictions

Generally permitted with notification:

  • Diabetes (insulin and supplies allowed)
  • Asthma and respiratory conditions
  • Heart conditions (stable, with medical clearance)
  • Arthritis and joint conditions
  • Mental health conditions (documented)
  • Blood clotting disorders (medical certificate required)

Restrictions or special requirements:

  • Recent surgery (typically 2-4 weeks required)
  • Pneumonia or respiratory infections
  • Unstable heart conditions (medical clearance needed)
  • Recent heart attack or stroke (doctor approval required)
  • Severe back problems with complications

📄 Medical Documentation Requirements

For medical conditions:

  1. Medical certificate from your doctor stating you are fit to fly
  2. List of medications and medical equipment needed
  3. Specific special requirements or accommodations
  4. Emergency contact and medical information
  5. Insurance and liability waiver (for some conditions)

Timing: Submit documentation 48 hours before departure for review

💊 Medications & Medical Equipment

What you can bring:

  • Prescription medications in original labeled bottles
  • Insulin and injection supplies
  • Inhalers and nebulizers
  • CPAP and sleep apnea devices
  • Oxygen concentrators (battery-powered, approved models)
  • Wheelchairs and mobility aids
  • Crutches and walking aids

Important: Keep medications in carry-on bag, not checked luggage

⭐ Mobility & Disability Services

  • Priority boarding for passengers with disabilities
  • Wheelchair accessibility at all gates and aircraft
  • On-board wheelchair assistance and transfers
  • Accessible lavatories and spacious seating
  • Guide dog accommodation (no quarantine periods)
  • Service dog in-cabin travel (no pet fees)
  • Assistance with boarding and deplaning
  • Special seating assignments (aisle, extra legroom)

💨 Oxygen Availability

Passengers needing oxygen:

  • MUST arrange oxygen with airline in advance (minimum 48 hours notice)
  • SmartFly provides bottled oxygen and delivery to destination (additional charge)
  • Passenger-supplied oxygen allowed (portable concentrators on approved list)
  • Medical documentation required from physician
  • Special seat assignment near lavatories for easier access

🙏 Booking with Special Medical Needs

  1. Contact Special Services BEFORE booking or during booking process
  2. Provide detailed information about your condition and needs
  3. Receive confirmation of available accommodations
  4. Submit medical documentation 48 hours before departure
  5. Receive call from medical coordinator to confirm arrangements
  6. Arrive at airport early (2.5-3 hours) for special procedures
