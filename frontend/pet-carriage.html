Pet Carriage Services

Travel with your furry friends safely and comfortably

🐾 Traveling with Pets on SmartFly

SmartFly welcomes your beloved pets as companions on your journey. We provide safe, comfortable, and climate-controlled accommodations for dogs, cats, rabbits, and other approved animals. Pets can travel in the cargo hold (checked) or in the cabin with you, depending on size and type.

🐠 Pet Categories & Options

🐶 Dogs & Cats (Cabin)

Max weight: 10 kg (22 lbs)

Fee: KES 3,500 per pet

Requirements: Airline-approved carrier, health certificate, valid vaccinations

🐇 Rabbits & Small Animals (Cabin)

Max weight: 5 kg (11 lbs)

Fee: KES 2,500 per pet

Requirements: Ventilated carrier, hay/bedding, health certificate

🐾 Dogs (Cargo Hold)

Max weight: Unlimited

Fee: KES 8,000 - 15,000 (by weight)

Requirements: Airline-approved crate, health cert, acclimation time

🐯 Exotic Pets (Cargo Hold)

Species: Birds, reptiles, others (case-by-case)

Fee: Contact special services

Requirements: CITES permit, health cert, special handling

📄 Required Documentation

  • Health certificate (issued within 10 days of travel by veterinarian)
  • Proof of rabies vaccination (if applicable)
  • Valid microchip registration details
  • Airline pet declaration form (available on booking)
  • Import/export permits (for international travel)
  • CITES certificate (for protected species)

💰 Pet Travel Costs

Cabin travel (small pets): KES 2,500 - 3,500 one-way

Cargo hold (large dogs): KES 8,000 - 15,000 depending on weight and distance

International flights: Add 20-30% to base domestic rates

Pet amenities package: KES 500 (includes water bowl, treats, comfort items)

🚪 Approved Carrier Requirements

  • Airline-approved carrier (IATA compliant for cargo)
  • Minimum dimensions: 24" length × 16" width × 14" height (for cabin)
  • Adequate ventilation (minimum 50% openings)
  • Secure locking mechanism
  • Absorbent padding and leak-proof bottom
  • Food and water bowls accessible
  • ID tag with pet name and contact information

🎒 Pre-Travel Preparation Checklist

4 weeks before: Schedule veterinary health check, obtain health certificate

2 weeks before: Ensure current vaccinations, microchip pet if not done

1 week before: Prepare carrier, pack supplies (food, medication, toys, blanket)

3 days before: Acclimate pet to carrier with practice trips

Day before: Light meal, ensure water access

Flight day: Arrive 2 hours early, carry recent photo of pet

⚠️ Restricted Breeds & Species

Note: SmartFly cannot transport:

  • Brachycephalic breeds in cargo (health risk: Bulldogs, Pugs, Persians)
  • Dangerous/aggressive animals
  • Snakes and venomous reptiles
  • Large primates and wild animals
  • Animals without proper health/import documentation

🌟 Service Animals & Emotional Support Animals

Certified service animals (guide dogs, hearing dogs, mobility assist): Travel FREE in cabin

Requirements: Service animal vest/certificate, handler documentation

Emotional Support Animals (ESA): Must follow standard pet policies and fees

Therapy animals: Contact special services for documentation requirements

🌄 Climate Considerations

SmartFly maintains temperature and humidity control in cargo holds. However, pets may experience stress during air travel. Consider:

  • Avoid peak heat seasons for cargo travel when possible
  • Provide sedation options (with veterinary approval)
  • Book early morning or late evening flights
  • Avoid connections - direct flights are less stressful
  • Include water bottle attachment in carrier

📅 Booking Your Pet's Flight

Steps to book:

  1. Contact Special Services at pets@smartfly.com or +254 700 000 002
  2. Provide pet details (species, breed, weight, age)
  3. Confirm carrier specifications and fees
  4. Book flight and pay pet travel fee
  5. Submit health certificate and required documents 7 days before
  6. Receive pet handling instructions and updates
