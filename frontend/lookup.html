Refunds & Cancellation Policy

Understand your refund options and cancellation policies for complete transparency.

Important Information

Refund eligibility depends on your ticket type (refundable vs non-refundable) and timing of cancellation. Always review your fare rules before requesting a refund to understand applicable fees and restrictions.

Refund Eligibility by Ticket Type

Ticket Type Cancellation Fee Refund Amount Processing Time
Refundable $0 - $50 Full or partial refund 7-14 business days
Non-Refundable Not eligible Taxes & fees only 14-21 business days
24-Hour Cancellation $0 100% refund 3-7 business days
Airline Cancelled $0 100% refund or rebooking 7-14 business days
