eVisa & Entry Requirements for Kenya

Essential travel documentation for visiting Kenya

📱 Kenya eVisa Overview

Kenya's eVisa system allows eligible travelers to obtain an electronic travel authorization online before arrival. SmartFly recommends obtaining your eVisa at least 3-5 business days before departure for a smooth journey.

💳 eVisa Requirements

📄 Required Documents

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity)
  • Return or onward ticket
  • Proof of accommodation
  • Email address for confirmation

💰 Costs

  • Single entry: USD 51
  • Multiple entry: USD 101
  • Processing: 3-5 business days
  • Valid for: 90 days

📸 Photo Requirements

  • Digital color photo
  • Recent (taken last 6 months)
  • Passport-size (4x6cm/35x45mm)
  • White/neutral background

👥 Eligibility & Exempt Nationalities

Citizens exempt from eVisa (on arrival): UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, and several others. Check official list at kenya.go.ke

Not eligible: Some nationalities require traditional visa. Contact Kenyan embassy for details

✈️ On Arrival at Kenya

  • Have your eVisa confirmation email and passport ready
  • Go to eVisa clearance lane (separate from traditional visa)
  • Scan your passport and biometrics
  • Receive entry stamp valid for 90 days
  • No need to carry physical visa document

🏨 Proof of Accommodation

Options accepted for eVisa:

  • Hotel reservation confirmation
  • Airbnb or vacation rental booking
  • Letter of invitation from host
  • Business hotel conference proof
  • Resort or safari lodge booking

⏱️ Processing Timeline

  • Instant processing: Usually approved within hours
  • Standard: 3-5 business days
  • Emergency: Available for urgent cases (contact support)
  • Apply as early as possible to avoid delays
  • Confirmation sent to your email

🚫 Visa Denial Reasons

Application may be denied if:

  • Passport expiring within 6 months
  • Criminal conviction or immigration violations
  • Health concerns (yellow fever, etc.)
  • Security concerns
  • Incomplete or fraudulent information

🏥 Health Requirements

Yellow Fever: Certificate required if traveling from endemic countries. Vaccination must be at least 10 days before arrival.

Recommended vaccinations: Typhoid, hepatitis A, malaria prophylaxis, COVID-19

Travel insurance: SmartFly recommends comprehensive travel insurance covering medical evacuation

💱 Currency & Money Matters

  • Currency: Kenyan Shilling (KES)
  • Exchange rate: Approximately 140 KES = 1 USD
  • Carry: Mix of cash, credit cards, and traveler's checks
  • ATMs: Available in major towns (Nairobi, Mombasa)
  • Credit cards: Visa/Mastercard accepted in most hotels

📍 Popular Entry Points

Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO), Nairobi: Main international hub with modern eVisa facilities

Mombasa International Airport (MBA): Coastal gateway with eVisa processing

Land borders: Tanzania, Uganda borders also accept eVisa
