Reduced Mobility Assistance

Comprehensive accessibility support for passengers with mobility challenges

⭐ SmartFly Accessibility Commitment

SmartFly is committed to providing accessible air travel for passengers with reduced mobility. Our airports, aircraft, and trained staff are prepared to support passengers with wheelchairs, walkers, mobility aids, or other physical limitations. Advance notification helps us provide the best support.

🚶 Mobility Assistance Services

🚗 Wheelchair & Conveyance

  • Airport wheelchairs at no cost
  • Personal wheelchair accommodation
  • Stair lift equipment available
  • Jet bridges for accessible boarding

🗂 Priority Check-in

  • Curbside check-in available
  • Reduced standing time
  • Special assistance at counter
  • Express line access

🌟 Boarding Assistance

  • Priority boarding (first group)
  • Wheelchair lift assistance
  • Aisle chair for narrow aisles
  • Personal escort through aircraft

🚰 Accessible Seating

  • Aisle seats available
  • Extra legroom accommodation
  • Movable armrests (some seats)
  • Proximity to lavatory

🚱 Lavatory Accessibility

  • Wheelchair-accessible lavatory on most aircraft
  • Grab bars and handrails
  • Spacious interior design
  • Crew assistance available

🏖️ Arrival Support

  • Disembarkation assistance
  • Wheelchair retrieval
  • Baggage handling help
  • Customs/immigration guidance

👦 Types of Mobility Conditions Supported

  • Wheelchair users (manual and electric)
  • Walkers and crutches
  • Canes and walking sticks
  • Scooters and motorized devices
  • Temporary injuries (casts, fractures)
  • Arthritis and joint pain
  • Muscular dystrophy and MS
  • Spinal injuries and paralysis
  • Amputees with prosthetics
  • Elderly passengers with mobility issues

📄 Booking & Notification

When booking:

  1. Select "Reduced Mobility" when searching flights
  2. Describe your mobility needs
  3. Indicate type of mobility aid (wheelchair, walker, etc.)
  4. Provide any special equipment requirements
  5. Receive confirmation of available services
  6. Team calls 48 hours before departure
  7. Confirm special arrangements

💱 Service Fees

Reduced Mobility Services: FREE (inclusive with all tickets)

Additional services:

  • Meet & Greet assistance: KES 3,500
  • Lounge access: KES 1,500
  • Ground transportation: KES 2,500-7,000 depending on vehicle
  • Companion ticket (free): One free companion ticket for caregiver

🇿🇫 Equipment Policies

Personal mobility aids transported:

  • Wheelchairs: Transported free of charge
  • Walkers/crutches: Carried in cabin or cargo hold
  • Electric scooters: Battery may need removal (safety regulations)
  • Prosthetics: Always allowed in cabin
  • Service animals: Allowed in cabin without restrictions

👥 Companion/Caregiver Policy

  • One free companion ticket for caregiver
  • Sitting together on flight guaranteed
  • Priority boarding together
  • Lounge access for companion
  • Assistance both receive from crew
  • No companion required if independent

🌟 Accessible Airport Information

Nairobi Hub (JKIA): Fully accessible with elevators, ramps, accessible restrooms, and trained staff

Major partner airports: London (LHR), Paris (CDG), Amsterdam (AMS), Dubai (DXB) all have comprehensive accessibility features

Airport maps: Available with accessible routes marked on booking confirmation
