Traveling with Infants & Toddlers

Make family travel comfortable and stress-free

👶 Baby-Friendly Flights with SmartFly

SmartFly is committed to making air travel with babies and toddlers as comfortable as possible. We offer special facilities, flexible seating, and comprehensive support for families traveling with young children.

👨‍👩‍👧 Age Guidelines & Eligibility

Infants (0-2 years): Can travel from 7 days old (with doctor's clearance). Held on parent's lap (free seat) or infant seat (own seat purchase)

Toddlers (2-5 years): Own seat required. Child seat required for take-off and landing in many countries

Young children (5-12 years): Standard seating, unaccompanied minor services available

🧷 Infant Seating Options

Lap infant (free): Hold baby on your lap throughout flight (not recommended by safety experts)

Infant seat (own seat): Child car seat or approved infant seat secured to seat - STRONGLY RECOMMENDED

Approved seats for aircraft: Check TSA/CAA approval before purchasing; infant seats with base work better on planes

📋 Booking Tips for Babies & Toddlers

  • Reserve bassinets and bulk head seats early (limited availability)
  • Request child meals when booking (must be ordered 24 hours before)
  • Notify airline if traveling with multiple infants or special needs
  • Choose direct flights when possible to minimize disruption
  • Book non-stop routes for younger babies (less cabin changes)
  • Request aisle seats for easier bathroom/diaper changes
  • Avoid red-eye flights with young children

🧻 What to Pack for Baby

  • Diapers (more than you think you'll need)
  • Wipes and diaper rash cream
  • Change of clothes (for baby and yourself)
  • Bottles, formula, breast pump
  • Medications (fever reducer, antihistamine)
  • Comfort items (favorite toy, blanket, pacifier)
  • Feeding bibs and burp cloths
  • Travel-sized toiletries (soap, lotion, shampoo)

⭐ Feeding on the Flight

Breastfeeding: Discrete feeding covers available; nursing rooms at major airports

Formula: Bring enough from home; you can request warm water from crew

Bottled water: Empty water bottles through security, refill at airport stations

Baby food: Purees and solid foods allowed; pre-order meals for toddlers with allergies

🤕 Managing Ear Pressure for Babies

  • Nurse or bottle-feed during take-off and landing (sucking equalizes pressure)
  • Use pacifier as backup for non-nursing babies
  • Consider infant pain reliever before flight (with doctor approval)
  • Avoid having baby sleep during descent
  • Keep baby upright and calm during pressure changes
  • Know that baby crying during descent is normal and helps equalize pressure

📄 Documentation for Babies & Toddlers

  • Birth certificate or passport for the child
  • Parent/guardian identification documents
  • Vaccination records (some countries require for entry)
  • Medical clearance letter (for babies under 14 days old)
  • Custody documents (if traveling with one parent)
  • Insurance documents
