🎯 Top Attractions

Canal Cruises

Iconic canals with 1,600 bridges. Peaceful boat tours through 17th-century waterways.

🏛️

Anne Frank House

Moving WWII memorial and museum in the hidden annex where Anne Frank wrote her diary.

🎨

Van Gogh Museum

World's largest collection of Vincent van Gogh paintings with 200+ masterpieces.

🏰

Dam Square

Historic center with Royal Palace, Madam Tussauds, and the iconic area's main attractions.

🌷

Spring tulip paradise with 7 million flowers. The world's largest flower garden.

Canal Houses

17th-century merchants' houses with distinctive gables lining the romantic canals.

    • 🚗 Getting Around

    • 🚲 Bikes: Most efficient way - Amsterdam is ultra-cycle friendly with dedicated lanes
    • 🚊 Tram: 17 tram lines covering the entire city efficiently
    • 🚢 Canal Boats: Romantic transport and sightseeing option
    • 🚶 Walking: Compact, pedestrian-friendly city perfect for exploring
    • 🚕 Taxis: Available but expensive - use other transport options

    💡 Travel Tips

    • ✓ EU citizens travel visa-free - check Schengen requirements
    • ✓ Rent a bike - cheapest and most authentic way to get around
    • ✓ Get an OV-chipkaart for trams and buses - discounted fares
    • ✓ Amsterdam Pass gives access to museums and public transport
    • ✓ Dutch people speak excellent English - communication is easy
    • ✓ Pickpocketing in crowded areas - secure your valuables
    • ✓ Tipping is optional - round up or add 5-10%
