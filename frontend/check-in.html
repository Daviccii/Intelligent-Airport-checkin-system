Online Check-In

Quick, easy, and convenient - check in in seconds

✓ Check-In Requirements

  • Check-in opens 48 hours before departure
  • Valid booking reference and passenger name
  • Applicable to most flights and routes
  • Complete check-in process before 2 hours before departure
  • For infants and special cases, contact customer service
💻

Online Check-In

Check in on our website using your booking reference and name. Get your boarding pass instantly.

📱

Mobile Check-In

Use the SmartFly Airways mobile app to check in on the go. Receive your mobile boarding pass.

🛫

Airport Check-In

📞

Call our customer service to check in by phone. Available for select routes and booking types.

Check-In Timeline

1

48 Hours Before Departure

Check-in becomes available. You'll receive an email notification when you can check in.

2

Complete Your Check-In

Select your seat (if applicable), add any special requests, and review passenger information.

3

Receive Boarding Pass

Get your digital boarding pass via email. You can also print it or use the mobile version.

4

2 Hours Before Departure

Proceed to the airport. Online check-in closes 2 hours before your flight departs.

5

Board Your Flight

Arrive at your gate according to the boarding announcement and present your boarding pass.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I check in if I have a connecting flight? +
You can check in online for your first flight only. For connecting flights, you'll need to check in at the airport or via the airline's system for the next leg.
What if I miss the online check-in deadline? +
If you miss online check-in, you can still check in at the airport at the check-in counter. However, there may be limited seat availability and potential delays.
Can I change my seat after checking in? +
Yes, you can request a seat change at the airport. Availability depends on the flight status. For paid seat upgrades, changes must be made before check-in.
Do I need to print my boarding pass? +
No, your mobile boarding pass is acceptable. You can show the digital version from your email or mobile app at security and boarding.
Can infants check in online? +
Infants traveling with a responsible adult can be included in online check-in. However, for special requirements or unaccompanied minors, contact our customer service team.
