Whether you're organizing a school trip, family reunion, sports team travel, or any group of 10 or more passengers, SmartFly offers customized group booking solutions with special rates, flexible payment terms, and dedicated support.

💵 Payment Options

Standard payment terms:

  • 30% deposit to secure booking
  • 50% payment 60 days before departure
  • 20% payment 30 days before departure
  • Alternative: Full payment upfront for 5% extra discount

Payment methods: Bank transfer, credit card, corporate billing, cash (for deposits)

📄 Required Documentation

  • Passenger manifest (names, dates of birth, contact info)
  • Group organizer/leader contact details
  • Special requests (dietary, medical, accessibility)
  • Passports for international groups (copies)
  • Visa documentation status
  • Baggage allowance preferences
  • Any special group arrangements needed

📅 Cancellation & Change Policy for Groups

Cancellation:

  • More than 90 days: 50% refund of payments
  • 60-90 days: 25% refund
  • 30-60 days: 10% refund
  • Less than 30 days: No refund (unless airline-caused)

Changes to manifest: Passenger additions/removals up to 15 days before at no penalty
