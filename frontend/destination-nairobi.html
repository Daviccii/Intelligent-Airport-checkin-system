🎯 Top Attractions

🦁

Nairobi National Park

The world's only wildlife park within a capital city. See lions, rhinos, and giraffes with Nairobi's skyline as backdrop.

🦒

Giraffe Centre

Hand-feed endangered Rothschild giraffes and learn about conservation efforts at this popular sanctuary.

🐘

David Sheldrick Elephant Orphanage

Watch baby elephants being fed and playing. Support elephant conservation through adoption programs.

🏛️

Nairobi National Museum

Explore Kenya's rich cultural and natural heritage through extensive collections and exhibitions.

🏞️

Karura Forest

Urban forest oasis with hiking trails, waterfalls, and picnic spots. Perfect for nature walks and cycling.

🛍️

Maasai Market

Vibrant markets selling authentic African crafts, jewelry, textiles, and souvenirs. Great for bargain hunters.

🚗 Getting Around

  • 🚕 Uber & Bolt: Convenient ride-hailing apps widely available
  • 🚌 Matatus: Colorful local minibuses for authentic experience
  • 🚖 Taxis: Metered taxis available at hotels and malls
  • 🚗 Car Rental: Recommended for safari trips and flexibility

💡 Travel Tips

  • ✓ Visa on arrival available for most nationalities (USD 50) or apply for eTA online
  • ✓ Yellow fever vaccination certificate required if coming from endemic areas
  • ✓ Carry small denominations of Kenyan Shillings for local markets
  • ✓ Traffic can be heavy - plan extra time for airport transfers
  • ✓ Altitude is 1,795m - stay hydrated upon arrival
  • ✓ English widely spoken in tourist areas and business districts
