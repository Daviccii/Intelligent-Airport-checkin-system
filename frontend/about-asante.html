About Asante Rewards

Africa's premier airline loyalty program

Our Story

Asante Rewards was launched in 2018 to recognize the loyalty of our valued passengers. The name "Asante" means "thank you" in Swahili, reflecting our commitment to rewarding our customers for choosing SmartFly Airways.

Over the years, we've grown to become Africa's most rewarding loyalty program, with millions of members earning and redeeming points on flights, hotels, dining, and more.
