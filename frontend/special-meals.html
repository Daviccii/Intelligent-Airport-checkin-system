🍽️ SmartFly Special Meal Services

SmartFly accommodates a wide variety of dietary requirements and preferences. From vegetarian and vegan to religious observances and medical restrictions, our catering partners prepare fresh, nutritious meals tailored to your needs. Order special meals when booking or up to 24 hours before departure.

  • Grains and pasta dishes

📈 Ordering Special Meals

How to request:

  1. During flight booking, select "Special Meal" option
  2. Choose your dietary requirement from list
  3. Add any additional notes/allergies
  4. Confirmation sent to your email
  5. Can modify until 24 hours before flight
  6. For medical conditions, attach doctor's note
  7. Meal prepared fresh and served hot

📄 Allergy & Medical Certification

For severe allergies or medical conditions:

  • Provide doctor's letter confirming allergy/condition
  • List all ingredients to avoid
  • Specify safe preparation requirements
  • Submit at least 5 days before travel
  • SmartFly verifies with medical team
  • Extra precautions taken during meal prep
  • Meal delivered in sealed, labeled packaging

🍟 In-Flight Snack Options

  • Fresh fruit available from galley
  • Nuts and trail mix (check allergen info)
  • Sandwiches and wraps
  • Desserts and pastries
  • Hot beverage selection (tea, coffee, chocolate)

🍕 Infant & Children Meals

  • Baby food (pureed) available for infants
