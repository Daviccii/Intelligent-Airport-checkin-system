Lost or Delayed Baggage

What to do if your baggage doesn't arrive with you

📄 Report Lost or Delayed Baggage

Important: Report missing baggage before leaving the airport. Go to the Baggage Service Office in the Arrivals area. Have your baggage tag and booking reference ready.

Baggage recovery rate: 99.2% of delayed baggage is recovered and delivered within 24-48 hours

🛍️ Emergency Assistance for Delayed Baggage

If your baggage is delayed, SmartFly provides immediate assistance:

  • Emergency toilet kit with toiletries (free)
  • Basic clothing items (t-shirt, underwear - free)
  • Medications and essential items reimbursement
  • Accommodation allowance if overnight delay
  • Meal allowance during delay
  • Phone credit for contacting family

💰 Reimbursement for Delayed Baggage Expenses

What SmartFly will reimburse:

  • Essential toiletries and cosmetics (receipts required)
  • Medication and medical supplies
  • Underwear and basic clothing
  • Accommodation for multi-day delays
  • Meals during baggage delay period
  • Reasonable ground transportation

Reimbursement limits: Up to KES 15,000 per day for delays under 72 hours

🏐 Compensation for Lost Baggage

Baggage Status Timeframe Maximum Compensation Action Required
Delayed 24 hours Within 24 hrs KES 15,000 Submit receipts for emergency items
Delayed 48-72 hours 2-3 days KES 30,000 File claim with documentation
Delayed 72+ hours 3+ days KES 50,000 Full claim assessment
Lost Baggage After 21 days USD 3,000 equivalent (per bag) Full inventory documentation required
Damaged Baggage Immediate USD 2,500 equivalent Photos of damage and repair estimates

📄 Documents Needed for Compensation Claims

  • Booking reference and flight details
  • Baggage tag/receipt numbers
  • Property Irregularity Report (PIR)
  • Itemized inventory of lost items with values
  • Purchase receipts for high-value items
  • Proof of payment (credit card statement)
  • Photos of items (if available)
  • Receipts for emergency purchases

📅 How to File a Claim

  1. Immediate action: File PIR at airport baggage service office
  2. Within 7 days: Submit formal claim with PIR number and documentation
  3. Claim submission: Email baggage-claims@smartfly.com with all documents
  4. Assessment: SmartFly reviews claim (5-10 business days)
  5. Decision: Compensation approved or requested additional documentation
  6. Payment: Funds transferred within 10 days of approval

🙋 Special Circumstances

Connecting flights: Baggage delayed to next destination - free rerouting included

Damaged during handling: Report immediately with photos - separate compensation process

Baggage never loaded: Automatically located and delivered to your address

Baggage in another city: We arrange return at no cost with expedited delivery

💮 Prevention Tips

Reduce baggage loss risks:

  • Use distinctive baggage tags and color markers
  • Add contact information inside and outside luggage
  • Take photos of your bags before departure
  • Keep receipts for expensive items
  • Avoid checking bags on tight connections
  • Check baggage allowance before packing
  • Use hardshell luggage for better protection
  • Consider baggage insurance for valuable items
