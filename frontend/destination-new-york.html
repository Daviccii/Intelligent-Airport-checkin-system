🎯 Top Attractions

🗽

Statue of Liberty

Iconic symbol of freedom. Take the ferry to Liberty Island and climb to the crown for panoramic views.

🏙️

Empire State Building

Legendary Art Deco skyscraper with observation decks offering 360° views of Manhattan.

🎭

Broadway & Times Square

World-famous theatre district with world-class musicals, shows, and neon-lit entertainment.

🏛️

Metropolitan Museum of Art

One of the world's greatest art museums with 2 million works spanning 5,000 years.

🌳

Central Park

843 acres of urban oasis with lakes, trails, meadows, and iconic landmarks like Bethesda Terrace.

🌉

Brooklyn Bridge

Historic suspension bridge with stunning Manhattan views. Walk across for unforgettable sunset views.

🚗 Getting Around

  • 🚇 Subway: 472 stations - fastest way to get around Manhattan and boroughs
  • 🚖 Yellow Cabs: Iconic taxis throughout the city, hail on street or use apps
  • 🚕 Ride-Hailing: Uber and Lyft widely available
  • 🚌 Buses: Extensive bus network covering all boroughs
  • 🚴 Citibike: Bike-sharing program with 20,000+ bikes at 1,300 stations

💡 Travel Tips

  • ✓ No visa required for most nationalities - ESTA for US entry
  • ✓ Get a MetroCard for subway/bus - much cheaper than taxis
  • ✓ NYC is walkable - comfortable shoes essential
  • ✓ Tipping is 15-20% in restaurants, 15% for taxis
  • ✓ Many museums offer "pay-what-you-wish" hours
  • ✓ Broadway shows - book in advance for best prices
