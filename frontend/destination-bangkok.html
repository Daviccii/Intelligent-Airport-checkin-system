🎯 Top Attractions

🏛️

Grand Palace

Magnificent royal residence with glittering spires. Home to Thailand's most sacred Buddha image.

🙏

Wat Pho

Temple of the Reclining Buddha - 46-meter golden Buddha. Tranquil and spiritually significant.

🛶

Floating Markets

Iconic Damnoen Saduak and Amphawa markets. Navigate wooden boats selling fruit and souvenirs.

🌙

Muay Thai

Watch legendary fighters at Rajadamnern or Lumpinee Stadium. Experience Thailand's national sport.

💆

Thai Massage

Traditional Thai massage centers offer authentic healing treatments at bargain prices.

🛍️

Chatuchak Market

World's largest weekend market with 15,000+ stalls. Browse crafts, clothing, food, and animals.

🚗 Getting Around

  • 🚇 BTS Skytrain: Fast, clean, elevated trains covering most main areas
  • 🚇 MRT Underground: Modern subway connecting central Bangkok
  • 🛵 Tuk-Tuk: Three-wheeled taxis - colorful and fun, negotiate price before riding
  • 🚕 Metered Taxi: Affordable, meter starts at 35 baht
  • ⛴️ Chao Phraya Express: River boats connecting neighborhoods along the river

💡 Travel Tips

  • ✓ Visa on arrival - 30 days visa-free for most nationalities
  • ✓ Show respect for the Thai Royal Family - it's a serious matter
  • ✓ Dress modestly when visiting temples - cover shoulders and knees
  • ✓ Thai people don't appreciate raised voices - be calm and friendly
  • ✓ The Baht is strong - budget 800-1000฿ daily for budget travelers
  • ✓ Street food is delicious and cheap - eat where locals eat
  • ✓ Don't touch people's heads or point feet - cultural taboos
