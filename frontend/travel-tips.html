Travel Tips & In-Flight Comfort

Make your flight experience more comfortable and enjoyable

✈️ Before Your Flight

  • Arrive 2-3 hours before departure for domestic flights
  • Arrive 3-4 hours early for international flights
  • Check baggage allowance and pack smartly
  • Select your seat online (extra legroom available)
  • Download airline app for real-time updates
  • Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing
  • Charge all electronic devices before boarding
  • Have important documents easily accessible

💡 In-Flight Comfort Tips

🎧 Entertainment

  • Personal entertainment screens
  • Movies, TV shows, music
  • Bring headphones/earbuds
  • Noise-cancelling recommended

💤 Sleep & Rest

  • Travel pillows provided
  • Blankets available on request
  • Window seats better for sleeping
  • Eye masks and earplugs helpful

💧 Hydration

  • Drink 1 cup water per hour
  • Avoid alcohol and caffeine
  • Free water available anytime
  • Moisturize skin frequently

🚶 Staying Active

  • Walk aisle every 1-2 hours
  • Do in-seat exercises (stretches)
  • Flex legs and feet regularly
  • Visit lavatory for movement

🍽️ Dining Options

  • Meal service included on long flights
  • Special meals available (pre-order)
  • Snacks available on demand
  • Complimentary beverages

😴 Jet Lag Management

  • Adjust sleep to destination time
  • Gradual light exposure helps
  • Stay hydrated for adjustment
  • Melatonin tablets available

🧘 Wellness During Flight

Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT) Prevention: Move legs frequently, wear compression socks, avoid prolonged immobility

Back Pain Relief: Lumbar support pillow, adjust seat recline, change position hourly

Headaches: Stay hydrated, avoid excess caffeine, take paracetamol as needed

Ear Discomfort: Chew gum during takeoff/landing, use nasal decongestant spray, yawn to equalize pressure

📱 Tech & Connectivity

  • SmartFly has WiFi on most flights (free for premium members)
  • USB charging ports available at most seats
  • Bluetooth connectivity for wireless headphones
  • Portable battery packs recommended
  • In-flight calling available (limited coverage)

🛫 Long-Haul Flight Tips

  • Book flights departing evening for better sleep
  • Upgrade to business class for extended comfort
  • Consider stopover options for rest breaks
  • Bring entertainment for 12+ hour flights
  • Compression socks reduce swelling
  • Medication for motion sickness available from crew
  • Business center available at major hubs

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Travel Comfort

  • Family seating available (request at booking)
  • Bassinets for infants on long flights
  • Kids meals and entertainment provided
  • Changing tables in lavatories
  • Child seats available for lap infants
  • Stroller assistance at gates

⚠️ What Not to Do

  • Don't leave carry-on under your feet
  • Avoid smoking (strictly prohibited)
  • Don't tamper with cabin equipment
  • Respect quiet hours on night flights
  • Don't recline seat excessively without notice
  • Avoid strong perfumes affecting others
  • Don't use electronic devices during takeoff/landing
