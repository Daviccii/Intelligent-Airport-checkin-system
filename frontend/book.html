⏰ Time To Think - Hold Your Fare

Not ready to commit? Hold your flight reservation at today's price while you finalize your plans.

How It Works

  1. Search and select your desired flight
  2. Choose "Hold My Fare" option at checkout
  3. Pay a small holding fee ($25-50)
  4. Your price is locked for up to 72 hours
  5. Complete purchase before hold expires
  6. Holding fee applies to final ticket price

Why Use Time To Think?

  • Lock in promotional fares
  • Coordinate with travel companions
  • Finalize vacation time with employer
  • Arrange accommodation and activities
  • Secure visa or travel documents
  • Peace of mind with flexible planning

📌 Important Details

Hold Duration:

Standard: 24 hours (free)
Extended: 72 hours ($25-50 fee)

Refund Policy:

If you don't complete purchase, holding fee is non-refundable. Your card won't be charged for the full ticket.

Availability:

Available on most routes. Not available during peak travel periods or for heavily discounted promotional fares.
