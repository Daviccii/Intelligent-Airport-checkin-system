Baggage Restrictions

Items you cannot bring on SmartFly flights

⚠️ Important Safety Notice

Safety is our top priority. We maintain strict baggage restrictions to protect all passengers and crew. Violation of these rules may result in:

  • Bag confiscation without compensation
  • Denial of boarding
  • Legal penalties and fines
  • Airline bans from future travel

🔥 Dangerous Goods (Absolutely Prohibited)

🧷 Explosives & Flammables

  • Fireworks and firecrackers
  • Dynamite and ammunition
  • Matches and lighters
  • Gasoline and paint thinner
  • Propane cartridges
  • Camping stoves with fuel

🔬 Weapons & Firearms

  • Guns, rifles, and pistols
  • Ammunition and cartridges
  • Crossbows and arrows
  • Tear gas and pepper spray
  • Knives over 6 inches
  • Martial arts weapons

⚗️ Corrosive Chemicals

  • Acids and alkalis
  • Drain cleaner and bleach
  • Battery acid
  • Paint stripper
  • Rust remover
  • Pool chlorine tablets

👻 Toxic & Biological

  • Pesticides and insecticides
  • Radioactive materials
  • Biological hazards
  • Cyanide compounds
  • Mercury and asbestos
  • Medical/clinical waste

🧪 Restricted Items (May Require Approval)

The following items may be allowed with special permissions and advance notice:

🐡 Lithium Batteries

  • Spare lithium ion batteries
  • Lithium metal batteries over 2g
  • Power banks over 20,000 mAh

Allowed: Batteries installed in devices

🐋 Electronics & Gadgets

  • Hoverboards (lithium battery)
  • E-cigarettes (with juice/batteries)
  • Drones (with batteries)
  • Power tools with large batteries

Allowed: When packed properly

🌈 Alcoholic Beverages

  • Liquor in carry-on bag
  • Open bottles or containers
  • More than 1 liter per person (checked)

Allowed: Duty-free sealed bottles in checked baggage

🧴 Medicinal & Sharp Items

  • Syringes without prescriptions
  • Large quantities of medication
  • Surgical instruments
  • Scalpels and lancets

Allowed: With documentation

🪠 Liquid & Gel Restrictions (3-1-1 Rule)

Carry-on baggage: Max 100 ml (3.4 oz) per container, 1 quart-sized bag total

Restricted liquids/gels:

  • Shampoo, conditioner, body wash
  • Toothpaste and cosmetics
  • Sunscreen and moisturizer
  • Hairspray and deodorant (aerosol)
  • Peanut butter and jam
  • Yogurt and pudding
  • Honey and sauces

📄 Item-by-Item Restrictions

Sharp Objects (carry-on prohibited): Knives, razors, scissors (over 6cm), nail clippers, ice picks, meat cleavers

Tools (carry-on prohibited): Hammer, drill, saw, wrench, pliers, screwdriver, power tools

Sporting Goods (carry-on prohibited): Baseball bat, golf club, ski poles, tennis racket, hockey stick

Compressed Gas (absolutely prohibited): Oxygen cylinder, propane, helium, nitrogen cartridges

Heavy/Awkward Items: Dumbbells, heavy power tools, large machinery (may exceed weight limits)

💶 Medical & Prescription Exceptions

SmartFly allows the following with proper documentation:

  • Prescription medications (original bottles with label)
  • Medical devices (CPAP, nebulizer, oxygen)
  • Insulin and injectable medications
  • Crutches, wheelchairs, walking aids
  • Wigs, prosthetics, mobility aids

Required: Doctor's letter or prescription for medications/devices. Notify airline 24 hours before departure.

🚳 International Travel Additions

Additional restrictions apply to international flights (beyond UK, EU, US restrictions):

  • Protected wildlife and endangered species items
  • Cultural artifacts and antiques (may require permits)
  • Certain electronics (may be restricted in destination country)
  • Foods and agricultural products (many countries prohibit)

Tip: Check destination country customs regulations before packing.

😨 What Happens if You Bring Prohibited Items

At security screening: Items will be confiscated and disposed of safely

At check-in: You may be denied boarding without refund

During flight: Criminal charges may apply for dangerous items

Fines: Government penalties up to KES 500,000 in Kenya, plus airline bans
