Important Information

⏰ Check-In Deadlines

Online Check-In: Opens 24 hours and closes 1 hour before departure
Airport Check-In: Opens 3 hours and closes 45 minutes before departure (international) / 30 minutes (domestic)

📄 Travel Documents

Ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months beyond your return date. Check visa requirements for your destination well in advance.

💳 Payment Methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, M-Pesa, and bank transfers. Changes and extras can be paid online or at the airport.
