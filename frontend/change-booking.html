Change Your Booking

Modify dates, passengers, or flight details easily

📌 Note: Changes are subject to availability and may incur additional fees. Modifications must be requested at least 2 hours before departure.

📅 Change Flight Date

Modify your departure date to any available date. Additional fees may apply if upgrading to a higher-priced date.

🔄 Change Flight

Switch to a different flight on the same route. Subject to availability and applicable fare differences.

👤 Change Passenger

Update passenger information such as name or title. Must be done 24 hours before departure.

➕ Add Passenger

Add additional passengers to your existing booking. Check availability and pricing for your flight.

How to Change Your Booking

1

Search Your Booking

Enter your booking reference and passenger name to locate your reservation.

2

Select Change Type

Choose what you want to modify - date, flight, passengers, or route.

3

Review Available Options

See available flights and pricing. Compare options before making your selection.

4

Calculate Fare Difference

Check if you need to pay additional fees or if you're eligible for a credit or refund.

5

Confirm Your Changes

Review and confirm your new booking details. You'll receive a confirmation email.
