🎯 Top Attractions

🏰

Tower of London

Historic castle housing the Crown Jewels. Witness 1000 years of history with the famous Beefeater guards.

🕰️

Big Ben & Parliament

London's iconic clock tower and the seat of British government along the River Thames.

🎡

London Eye

Giant observation wheel offering breathtaking 360° views of London's skyline.

🏛️

British Museum

World-class museum with 8 million works including the Rosetta Stone and Egyptian mummies. Free entry.

👑

Buckingham Palace

The Queen's official residence. Watch the famous Changing of the Guard ceremony.

West End Theatres

World-renowned theatre district with musicals, plays, and performances rivaling Broadway.

🚗 Getting Around

  • 🚇 The Tube: Extensive underground network - fastest way around central London
  • 🚌 Double-Decker Buses: Iconic red buses covering all areas, great for sightseeing
  • 🚂 Overground & DLR: Above-ground trains connecting outer zones
  • 🚖 Black Cabs: Famous London taxis available at stands and by hailing
  • 🚴 Boris Bikes: Cycle hire scheme with docking stations citywide

💡 Travel Tips

  • ✓ Get an Oyster Card or use contactless payment for public transport - much cheaper than single tickets
  • ✓ Many museums and galleries offer free entry (British Museum, National Gallery, Tate Modern)
  • ✓ Stand on the right on escalators - Londoners walk on the left
  • ✓ Book West End theatre tickets in advance or try TKTS booth for same-day discounts
  • ✓ Weather is unpredictable - always carry an umbrella
  • ✓ Most shops close by 6pm on weekdays, 8pm on Thursdays in central areas
