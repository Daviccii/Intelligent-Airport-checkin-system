🎯 Top Attractions

🏙️

Burj Khalifa

World's tallest building at 828m. Visit the observation deck on the 124th floor for stunning views.

🛍️

Dubai Mall

World's largest shopping mall with 1,200 stores, aquarium, ice rink, and fountain shows.

🏝️

Palm Jumeirah

Iconic man-made island shaped like a palm tree with luxury resorts and beaches.

🕌

Dubai Creek & Old Dubai

Historic heart of Dubai with traditional souks, abra boats, and authentic Arabian atmosphere.

🏖️

Jumeirah Beach

Pristine white sand beaches with views of Burj Al Arab and water sports activities.

🏜️

Desert Safari

Thrilling dune bashing, camel rides, falconry, and Bedouin-style dinner under the stars.

🚗 Getting Around

  • 🚇 Dubai Metro: Modern, driverless trains - Red & Green lines cover major areas
  • 🚕 Taxis: Abundant, metered, and affordable - starting at AED 12
  • 🚌 Dubai Bus: Extensive network connecting all areas
  • 🚗 Car Rental: Well-maintained roads, but traffic can be heavy
  • ⛴️ Abra: Traditional water taxis across Dubai Creek (AED 1)

💡 Travel Tips

  • ✓ Visa on arrival for most nationalities - free 30-90 day stays
  • ✓ Dress modestly in public areas - shoulders and knees covered
  • ✓ Summer (Jun-Sep) is extremely hot - indoor activities recommended
  • ✓ No tipping required but 10% appreciated for good service
  • ✓ Dubai is very safe - one of the world's safest cities
  • ✓ Alcohol only served in licensed hotels and restaurants
  • ✓ Friday is the weekend day - some businesses closed
