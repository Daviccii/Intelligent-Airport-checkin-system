Hand Baggage Allowance

Travel light and smart with our flexible hand baggage policies

👜 Hand Baggage Basics

Hand baggage (also called carry-on luggage) travels with you in the aircraft cabin. SmartFly allows 1 piece of hand baggage per passenger at no extra cost, plus a personal item (backpack, laptop bag, purse).

📏 Size & Weight Limits

Economy Class

Max 56 × 45 × 25 cm

Weight: 8 kg (17.6 lbs)

Quantity: 1 piece + 1 personal item

Examples: Roller bag, small suitcase

Premium Economy

Max 56 × 45 × 25 cm

Weight: 8 kg (17.6 lbs)

Quantity: 1 piece + 1 personal item

Extra: 1 additional small bag allowed

Business Class

Max 62 × 45 × 25 cm

Weight: 10 kg (22 lbs)

Quantity: 2 pieces + 1 personal item

Extra: Coat and umbrella free

First Class

Max 68 × 45 × 25 cm

Weight: 12 kg (26.4 lbs)

Quantity: 2 pieces + 1 personal item

Extra: Coat, umbrella, hat box complimentary

✓ Allowed Items in Hand Baggage

  • Laptop and tablet (in cabin baggage)
  • Toiletries bag (limited quantities)
  • Phone, chargers, and electronics
  • Medications (with prescription if required)
  • Glasses/sunglasses and contacts
  • Small books or documents
  • Baby items (bottle, formula, diapers)
  • Duty-free alcohol (sealed, in duty-free bag)
  • Non-alcoholic beverages (empty bottles, refill after security)
  • Travel essentials (pillow, blanket, eye mask)

✗ Prohibited Items in Hand Baggage

  • Sharp objects (knives, scissors, multi-tools)
  • Liquids over 100ml (3.4 oz)
  • Sporting goods (bats, clubs, guns)
  • Explosives and flammables
  • Aerosols (except deodorant/hair spray - limited)
  • Tools and equipment
  • Firearms and ammunition
  • Lithium batteries (except personal devices)
  • Hazardous materials
  • Heavy objects that violate weight limits

💡 Smart Packing Tips

Layer strategically: Pack heavier items at the bottom, light items on top

Use compression bags: Maximize space and minimize wrinkles

Roll clothing: Takes less space than folding

Keep essentials accessible: Medications, chargers, travel documents on top

Follow 3-1-1 rule: Liquids max 3.4 oz, 1 quart-size bag per person

Weigh before airport: Avoid overweight baggage charges

Keep receipt: Duty-free purchases must stay in sealed bag

🔧 Baggage Not Meeting Requirements

If your hand baggage exceeds size or weight limits at check-in, you may be asked to:

  • Check the bag into hold (may incur additional charges)
  • Remove and repack items to fit within limits
  • Pay excess baggage fees

Tip: Arrive early to allow time for possible rebooking of oversized bags.

👶 Special Categories

Infants (0-2 years): Infant seat and baby bag allowed free of charge

Mobility aids: Wheelchairs and walking aids travel free as hand baggage

Medical equipment: CPAP machines, oxygen, inhalers allowed (notify in advance)

Musical instruments: Allowed if dimensions fit baggage sizer; fragile items should be soft cases
