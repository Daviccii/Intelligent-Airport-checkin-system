Pregnancy Travel Guide

Safe and comfortable air travel during pregnancy

🧀 Flying While Pregnant

SmartFly welcomes pregnant passengers and provides special accommodations to ensure safe, comfortable travel. Most airlines allow flying until 36 weeks of pregnancy. Plan ahead and notify us of your pregnancy when booking or managing your reservation.

💪 Pregnancy Timeline & Flying

First Trimester (0-12 weeks): Generally safe to fly. Nausea and fatigue common. Extra rest recommended.

Second Trimester (13-26 weeks): Best time to fly. Energy levels higher, lowest miscarriage risk. Book longer trips during this period.

Third Trimester (27-36 weeks): Flying permitted but discomfort increases. Extra legroom seats recommended. Consult doctor before booking.

After 36 weeks: No commercial flight allowed. Risk of labor during flight makes travel inadvisable.

💊 Medical Clearance & Documentation

Required from doctor:

  • Medical fitness to fly certificate
  • Estimated delivery date
  • Confirmation of single/multiple pregnancy
  • Any pregnancy complications or restrictions
  • Contact details of healthcare provider

Timing: Submit documentation at time of booking or 48 hours before departure

🌟 Special Accommodations Available

💺 Extra Legroom Seating

  • Bulkhead or exit row seats
  • Free upgrade for pregnant passengers
  • Easier aisle access
  • Lavatory proximity

🌼 Comfort Items

  • Maternity pillows provided
  • Blankets and cushions
  • Anti-nausea ginger tea
  • Compression socks available

💳 Priority Check-in

  • First in line at counter
  • Less standing time
  • Assistance with luggage
  • Special help at security

🍽️ Customized Meals

  • Mild/bland meal options
  • Extra snacks for cravings
  • Frequent beverages
  • Ginger or anti-nausea options

📩 Cabin Crew Attention

  • Frequent check-ins by crew
  • Assistance with lavatory
  • Monitoring for complications
  • Medical personnel on standby

💍 Personal Assistance

  • Designated crew member assigned
  • Call button priority responses
  • Extra bathroom breaks allowed
  • Emotional support available

🤕 Health & Wellness Tips

  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing
  • Move around cabin every hour to prevent blood clots
  • Stay hydrated (avoid caffeine and alcohol)
  • Use support stockings/compression socks
  • Elevate feet when seated
  • Take frequent restroom breaks
  • Have snacks for nausea management
  • Avoid sleeping flat on back after 24 weeks

🚫 Things to Avoid

  • Flights exceeding 6 hours (if over 32 weeks)
  • Flights departing more than 2 hours from medical facilities
  • Multiple connections (tiring and stressful)
  • High-altitude destinations (thin oxygen)
  • Destinations with Zika or malaria (pregnancy risks)
  • Destinations with poor medical infrastructure
  • Travel if having pregnancy complications

📈 Packing for Pregnancy Travel

Essentials to bring:

  • Prenatal records and ultrasound images
  • Doctor's clearance letter
  • Insurance documentation
  • Maternity compression socks
  • Anti-nausea medication (ginger)
  • Prenatal vitamins
  • Comfortable pillow and blanket
  • Extra underwear and maternity pads
  • Change of clothes for emergencies
