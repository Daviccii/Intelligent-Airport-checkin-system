🤝 What is Codesharing?

Codesharing allows SmartFly to offer flights operated by our partner airlines under our flight designations. You book an SF ticket but may travel on a partner airline's aircraft. All codeshare flights maintain our high service standards.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Will I know if my flight is a codeshare? A: Yes, your confirmation will clearly state if it's a codeshare. The flight number may start with 'SF' but the aircraft will have the partner's name.

Q: Do codeshare flights cost more? A: No, codeshare fares are the same as SmartFly-operated flights. Pricing is competitive.

Q: Can I change my booking if it's a codeshare? A: Yes, same flexibility applies. Changes subject to your ticket type and partner airline policies.

Q: Do I get miles on codeshare flights? A: Yes, all miles earned when booking under SF flight code apply to your account.
