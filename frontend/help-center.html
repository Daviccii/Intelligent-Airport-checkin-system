Frequently Asked Questions

What are your booking cancellation policies?

+

Cancellation policies vary based on ticket type. Refundable tickets can be cancelled up to 24 hours before departure for a full refund. Non-refundable tickets can be converted to travel credits valid for 12 months. For schedule changes by SmartFly, you're eligible for a full refund or rebooking at no cost.

How do I update my personal information?

+

You can update your personal information by logging into your account and visiting the "Profile" section. For changes to your passport or visa information in a booking, contact our customer service team at least 24 hours before departure.

What is the baggage allowance on my flight?

+

Baggage allowance depends on your ticket type and route. Typically, Economy passengers get 1 checked bag (up to 23kg) and 1 carry-on. Business and First Class passengers receive 2-3 checked bags plus additional carry-on allowance. You can view your specific allowance in your booking confirmation.

How do I earn Asante Rewards points?

+

Members earn 1 point per KES spent on flights. Additional points can be earned through partner hotels, car rentals, dining, and shopping. Points vary by membership tier, with higher tiers earning bonus multipliers. Check your booking for the exact points value.

Can I change my flight after booking?

+

Yes, you can change your flight through "Manage Booking" on our website or app. Changes are subject to availability and applicable fare differences. Some restrictions may apply, such as minimum notice requirements or blackout dates. Contact us for assistance.

What happens if my flight is delayed or cancelled?

+

In case of cancellation, we'll rebook you on the next available flight or offer a full refund. For delays, we provide care and assistance. EU 261/2004 compensation may apply for eligible flights. Check our "Delays & Cancellations" page for detailed information and your rights.

Still need help?

Our customer service team is available 24/7 to assist you.
