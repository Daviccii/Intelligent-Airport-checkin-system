eTA for Travel in Kenya

Most travelers to Kenya require an electronic Travel Authorization (eTA). Apply online at least 3 days before your departure:

  • Visit the official Kenya eTA portal
  • Complete the online application form
  • Upload required documents (passport copy, photo)
  • Pay the application fee (approx. $30-50 USD)
  • Receive eTA approval via email within 1-3 business days

Requirements: Valid passport (6+ months validity), return ticket, proof of accommodation, yellow fever vaccination certificate (if coming from endemic areas).

Comfort & Travel Tips

Before Your Flight

  • Check-in online 24 hours before departure
  • Arrive at airport 3 hours before international flights
  • Pack essentials in carry-on (medications, valuables, documents)
  • Stay hydrated and avoid heavy meals before flying

During Your Flight

  • Drink water regularly to stay hydrated
  • Move around every 2-3 hours to prevent DVT
  • Use noise-canceling headphones for better rest
  • Adjust your watch to destination time zone

Long-Haul Comfort Tips

  • Wear comfortable, layered clothing
  • Bring neck pillow and eye mask
  • Consider compression socks for circulation
  • Request aisle seat for easier movement
