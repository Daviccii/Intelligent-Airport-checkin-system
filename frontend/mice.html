🎯 What is MICE?

MICE encompasses business travel for meetings, incentive trips, conventions, and exhibitions. SmartFly provides comprehensive solutions tailored to the specific needs of corporate groups traveling for business events and professional development.

💰 Pricing & Discounts

SmartFly offers competitive group fares based on group size and frequency:

  • 10-25 passengers: 5-8% discount
  • 26-50 passengers: 8-12% discount
  • 51-100 passengers: 12-16% discount
  • 100+ passengers: Custom pricing (contact sales team)
  • Frequent travelers: Additional loyalty rewards
  • Advance bookings: Early bird discounts available
